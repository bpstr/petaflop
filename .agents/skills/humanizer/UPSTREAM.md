## Local changes

The adaptation condenses the upstream pattern catalogue and examples into a magazine-editing workflow. It preserves the fact-preservation and prose-only file-editing safeguards while adding explicit Hungarian and technical-review requirements.

It does not import English-specific punctuation or hyphenation rules wholesale. Hungarian quotation marks, meaningful numeric ranges, and purposeful dashes remain valid. Real uncertainty, dated availability, code, attribution, commercial eligibility, and version restrictions must survive editing. News comparisons with earlier versions remain valid. No first-person experience, opinion, or reaction may be fabricated to add personality.

It omits claims that stylistic patterns establish authorship and makes no detector-evasion promise. It returns only final prose in the publishing workflow rather than inserting its drafting process into the magazine.

## Updating

Review an upstream diff at a specific commit rather than installing whatever happens to be on `main`. Preserve the upstream license and attribution, update the pinned revision and local version only when actually reviewed, and document which changes were adopted or rejected. Do not automatically overwrite the Hungarian safeguards. Use the [editorial review cases](../magazine-writing/references/review-cases.md) to check factual preservation after changes; describe any executed model evaluation separately from a manual document review.
